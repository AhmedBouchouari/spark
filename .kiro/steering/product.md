# Product: Spark Growth System

Spark is a client-acquisition service (marketed as a "growth system") targeting health and beauty professionals in Morocco:

- **Salons de beauté** (beauty salons)
- **Médecins esthétiques** (aesthetic medicine clinics)
- **Cabinets de kinésithérapie** (physiotherapy practices)

The core value proposition is converting local ad visibility into confirmed appointments delivered directly via WhatsApp, at a flat monthly fee of 1 500 DH with no long-term commitment.

## Key Concepts

- **WhatsApp-first**: all conversion funnels end with a WhatsApp message, not a form or funnel
- **No-contract**: monthly subscription, cancellable anytime
- **Done-for-you**: Spark handles strategy, ad creation, targeting, and follow-up
- **Local targeting**: geographic and interest-based ad targeting for each practice's area

## Target Market

Morocco — French and Arabic speaking audience. The site is bilingual (FR / AR) with RTL support for Arabic.
